Why one central clearer changes everything: netting

The same maths that makes DTCC work for shares is now coming to the world's biggest bond market.

Daily US equity and corporate bond activity, gross~$3.0 trillion
What actually has to move after NSCC nets it$30 to 60 billion

Netting cancels opposite trades against each other, so only the difference settles. It cuts payment obligations by about 98%. That is why trades cannot simply settle one by one, instantly: there is not enough money in the system to move the gross amount. (Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City, August 2026, citing DTCC.)

How we got here

Five dates that explain the visit.

13 DEC 2023
The SEC adopts the Treasury clearing ruleMost Treasury trades, still cleared firm-to-firm, must move to a central counterparty.
28 MAY 2024
US settlement moves to T+1DTCC co-led the industry programme. Money freed up: NSCC's clearing fund fell about $3 billion, and 95% of trades are now affirmed by 9pm on trade day.
29 JUN 2026
NSCC clearing goes 24 by 5The guarantee now covers overnight trades, Sunday 8pm to Friday 8pm, as exchanges move towards near-24-hour trading.
31 DEC 2026
Treasury cash deadlineCash purchases and sales must be submitted for central clearing. About seven weeks after the DTCC site visit on 11 November 2026.
30 JUN 2027
Treasury repo deadlineAll Treasury repo and reverse repo with a direct participant follows.
