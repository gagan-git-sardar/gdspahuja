Why this matters

In December 2025, a Consumer Reports and Groundwork Collaborative investigation found that Instacart's AI-driven pricing experiments meant most shoppers were quietly paying different prices for identical groceries. The FTC followed with a $60 million settlement and an investigation into the pricing tool behind it. Instacart responded by ending item price tests altogether and promising the same price to everyone at the same store and time.

That promise is only worth something if shoppers can check it. TrueBasket is a concept for the transparency layer that promise needs: one honest total, every fee named, every price difference explained in plain language. The True Cost Score rewards low markups, low fee load, and, above all, prices that do not change depending on who is asking.
