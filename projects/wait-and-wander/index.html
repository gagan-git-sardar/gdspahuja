The problem

A long wait is dead time, and leaving feels risky

57 minutes

What NYC diners will wait for a walk-in table, the longest of any US city. OpenTable 2026 Dining Trends, via Time Out.

39 minutes

The US average willingness to wait. New York is the outlier, not the norm. OpenTable 2026 Dining Trends.

72%

Of diners say they will not wait more than 30 minutes for a table. Toast diner survey, 2024.

Fear 1: "If I wander off, I lose my table." So most people stay on the sidewalk, close to the host stand.
Fear 2: "I don't know this area." They don't know what is nearby, or whether it fits the time they have.

People don't need the wait to disappear. They need to trust that they can use it.

How it works

Three steps, no app download drama

1

Join a line

Put your name down as usual. WaitLess shows your live wait and holds your place on the restaurant's own list.

2

Wander with a code word

Nearby cafes, shops and galleries that fit your wait, walking time and a safety buffer included. Visit with a code word and the offer is yours.

3

Get called back

When it is time to walk back, you get the alert. When your table is ready, you are already there.

The fairness promise

Leaving never moves you back

Your place is held by the restaurant's list, not by where you happen to stand. Wandering does not move you up, and it never moves you down. Nobody can pay to skip, and nobody loses a spot for using their wait well.

The line stays fair for everyone in it.

What we heard

Findings from the field

Business model

How WaitLess earns

1

Launch free for everyone

No charge for people waiting, no charge for businesses. The only goal at the start is users.

2

Show ads once the audience is big enough

A free app with thousands of local users can carry local ads.

3

Prove the lift

We track how much sales rise at a cafe or shop after we start sending it waiters. That proof is our bargaining power.

4

Charge for traffic

With proof in hand, cafes and shops pay a fee for the customers we redirect to them. Later, a premium tier on the customer side.

Questions

Fair questions

Do I lose my place if I leave?

No. Your place sits on the restaurant's list, not on the sidewalk. WaitLess shows it as protected the whole time you are away, and the head-back alert fires with walking time to spare.

What if I am late getting back?

Places only appear if they fit your wait with a 10 minute buffer built in, and they drop off the list as your time runs down. The alert tells you to head back before it is urgent.

Can I pay to skip the line?

No. Never. WaitLess does not sell position in any line. It only helps you use the wait you already have.

What does it cost?

Nothing for people waiting, and nothing for businesses during launch. The earning model comes later, and it is set out openly in the research section above.
