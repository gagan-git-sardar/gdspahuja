ClaimFlow

ClaimFlow sorts new insurance claims, built for the NYU x Capgemini Hackathon. It reads each claim, checks how serious it is, checks for problems, and sends the simple ones forward for fast handling. Anything severe, incomplete or suspicious stops at a human. Nothing is decided automatically.
